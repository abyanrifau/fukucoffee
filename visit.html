Good to Know

A few small things.

Seating

Window counter, a few two-tops, and one long shared table. Around twenty-two seats. We don't take table bookings for groups under five.

Getting here

A few minutes on foot from the ferry terminal and the main bus stops. Bicycle parking is out front; car parking is on the street.

Wi-Fi & laptops

Wi-Fi is on, and laptops are welcome on weekdays. On weekend mornings we ask that the window seats stay screen-free.
